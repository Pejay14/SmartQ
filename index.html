01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making Registrar Services More Organized

SmartQ is a proposed web-based queue reservation and document tracking system intended for the University Registrar's Office of JRMSU.

01
◷

Queue Reservation

Students can select a Registrar service, reserve a queue slot, and monitor their queue position.

02
◫

Document Tracking

Users can monitor the status of requested physical documents from submission until completion.

03
◉

Registrar Dashboard

Authorized Registrar personnel can manage queues, document requests, statuses, and reports.

!
Project Focus

The system focuses on improving the organization and visibility of Registrar services without requiring students to upload their physical document requirements.

02 — PROJECT GOALS

What SmartQ Aims to Achieve

The project is structured around practical improvements to Registrar service management.

01

Reduce Unnecessary Waiting

Provide students with scheduled queue slots and queue position information.

02

Improve Service Visibility

Allow students to monitor their queue and document request status.

03

Organize Registrar Operations

Provide Registrar personnel with a centralized management dashboard.

04

Support Better Planning

Generate useful queue and document management information for the office.
