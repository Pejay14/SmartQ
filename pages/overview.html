01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making Registrar Services More Organized

SmartQ is a proposed web-based queue reservation and document tracking system intended for the University Registrar's Office of JRMSU.

01
◷

Queue Reservation

Students can select a Registrar service, reserve a queue slot, and monitor their queue position.

02
◫

Document Tracking

Users can monitor the status of requested physical documents from submission until completion.

03
◉

Registrar Dashboard

Authorized Registrar personnel can manage queues, document requests, statuses, and reports.

!
Project Focus

The system focuses on improving the organization and visibility of Registrar services without requiring students to upload their physical document requirements.
