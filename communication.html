08 — PROJECT COMMUNICATION PLAN

Keeping the Qnnect Project Connected

This plan explains how the project team will share updates, gather stakeholder input, coordinate work, and record decisions for the proposed JRMSU Registrar Queue and Document Tracking System.

Communication Objectives

OBJECTIVE 01

Keep the project team informed

Share progress, task assignments, upcoming activities, and blockers so team members can coordinate their work.

OBJECTIVE 02

Confirm stakeholder needs

Gather and clarify Registrar workflow requirements and end-user needs for queue reservations and document tracking.

OBJECTIVE 03

Collect feedback at key milestones

Present proposed interfaces and project outputs to relevant stakeholders and use their feedback to guide refinements.

OBJECTIVE 04

Maintain clear project records

Document decisions, requirements, meeting outcomes, and testing issues so the team can follow up consistently.

Types and Forms of Communication

WRITTEN · ASYNCHRONOUS

Group chat

For brief progress updates, reminders, announcements, and quick coordination between meetings.

VERBAL · SYNCHRONOUS

Weekly group meeting

For reviewing progress, discussing blockers, assigning follow-up tasks, and confirming team decisions.

VERBAL · DIRECT

Interviews and consultations

For gathering requirements and clarifying Registrar processes and end-user needs.

WRITTEN · FEEDBACK

Feedback form or survey

For collecting structured comments on user needs and proposed queue or document-tracking features.

WRITTEN · PROJECT RECORD

Shared project documents

For keeping meeting notes, requirements, design feedback, testing issues, and project decisions accessible to the team.
